Lowest-confidence sheet in this set. Unlike SEC (PDF fill operators), Ampol (client meta tag) or CleanCo (PDF + raster), these hexes come from a single logo-aggregator that sampled the SVG. EA's own site publishes no theme-color and their Brandfetch profile has no colour data. Corroborating signal only: their asset filenames are chevron-down-green.svg. Verify before building.

Good news on contrast — a first for this set. #007D2F is 5.28:1 on white, so unlike SEC's orange, Ampol's red or CleanCo's cyan, EA's brand colour can actually carry body text. Both anchors pass AA on white and reversed.

Accent scale

This is not a true accent. #008D36 is the third sampled green and sits almost on top of green-600 — it adds brightness, not a second hue. EA appears to be a single-chroma brand. Never pair it with green-950 (2.37:1, fails), and never use it for text (4.32:1, fails AA). A genuine second hue is the single most valuable thing to obtain from this client.

Cautions

Success is deliberately teal, not green. Green is the brand here, so a green success state reads as chrome rather than a signal — the mirror image of Ampol's red problem. #005E7A (7.29:1) is far enough in hue to be unambiguous. Positive and negative values must still carry a sign or arrow, never colour alone.

Chart series — fixed order

One hue, so this is a sequential ladder, not a categorical set. Series 1–4 are green steps separated by luminance; series 5 is neutral. Fine for stacked or ordered data, poor for unrelated categories. Dash patterns are mandatory. Past three categories, request an extended data-viz palette.
