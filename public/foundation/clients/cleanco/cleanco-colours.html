Brand anchors

CleanCo cyan
#64D0E4 · cyan-300
CleanCo charcoal
#4F4C4D · char-600

The brand cyan is 1.80:1 on white. That is the lowest of any client in this system — it fails AA for text and misses the 3:1 non-text threshold by a wide margin. It cannot carry text, icons, thin lines, chart marks, or white text on top of it. Treat #64D0E4 as a large-area fill only.

Cyan on charcoal is the one readable brand pairing (4.72:1) — and it is exactly how the annual report uses it, for headings on the dark panel. On a dark surface, cyan can carry text. On white, use cyan-700 #326872 (6.25:1) instead.

Neutrals are warm here, not the shared cool set. #4F4C4D and #EDEDED are lifted straight from the report; the rest are derived from them. Don't mix in the cool Radix greys used for SEC and Ampol — the temperature clash is visible side by side.

Chart series — fixed order

This palette has one hue. CleanCo has no second chromatic anchor — the report is cyan plus charcoal and nothing else — so the series below is a sequential ladder, not a categorical set. It reads well for stacked or ordered data and poorly for unrelated categories. Dash patterns are mandatory. Anything past three categories needs an extended data-viz palette from the client.
