Best-sourced sheet in the client set. Twelve of the twenty-six values below are lifted directly from fill operators in the FY2025 annual report — five blues, one cyan and six greys — and corroborated by pixel sampling of the rendered pages. Only the tints and the darkest step are derived.

The palette is a single blue ramp plus a full grey family. Unlike the other clients, CS Energy publishes a complete neutral scale of its own (#231F20 through #EEF1F2), so this sheet uses their greys rather than the shared Tally set. Don't substitute.

Cyan is 2.53:1 on white and 2.70:1 on the deep blue. It fails against both surfaces, so it can't sit on either as text or as a thin mark. Use acc-300 #8CDBF8 when you need the accent readable on blue chrome.

No semantic collision here — a first. SEC's orange, Ampol's red, EnergyAustralia's and SUPA's green all doubled as signal colours. A blue brand leaves red, green and amber free, so the standard semantic set works unmodified. Only info needs care: use blue-800 itself rather than a separate token.

Chart series — fixed order

One hue plus a neutral, so this is a sequential ladder. Luminance separation is wide (6.83 → 1.76), which makes it read well stacked or ordered. Dash patterns remain mandatory for categorical use, and #6DCFF6 is a fill, never a thin line.
